[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]
Tired of the clunky, generic mod menus that feel completely out of place in Fallout Shelter?

This mod completely transforms the in-game cheat and utility experience into an authentic, immersive [b]RobCo Pip-Boy 3000 Terminal[/b]. Featuring [b]10 customizable Fallout faction & holiday themes[/b], smooth interactive button glows, dynamic resolution scaling, and [b]full bilingual support[/b] (English & Turkish with instant one-click switching).

In addition to visual polish, this mod fixes critical engine crashes from legacy mods (such as the notorious freeze when leveling large vaults), introduces a built-in background [b]Quest Automation engine[/b], and natively integrates [b]V.A.T.S. (Vault Accelerated Time System)[/b] for instant time control with smart auto-pausing.

[size=4][b]How to Use in Game[/b][/size]
[list]
[*] Press [b]F4[/b] on your keyboard to toggle the mod menu at any time.
[*] Alternatively, click the [b][ ⚙ MOD MENU (F4) ][/b] terminal button located just below your dweller count icon in the top left.
[*] Click [b]LANG: EN / DİL: TR[/b] in the top-right corner of the menu to swap languages instantly.
[*] Click [b]◄[/b] or [b]►[/b] on the theme bar to switch between the 10 Fallout faction themes.
[*] Use the [b]V.A.T.S.[/b] tab to toggle time acceleration, or use [b]Ctrl + Alt[/b] / [b]Shift + Alt[/b].
[/list]
