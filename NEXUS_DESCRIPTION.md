# Pip-Boy Terminal Mod Menu & Ultimate Cheats (10 Themes & Bilingual)
The definitive Pip-Boy CRT terminal overhaul, integrated V.A.T.S. time acceleration, automated questing, crash-free dweller leveling, and 47+ integrated cheats for Fallout Shelter.

---

## Description
Tired of the clunky, generic mod menus that feel completely out of place in Fallout Shelter?

This mod completely transforms the in-game cheat and utility experience into an authentic, immersive **RobCo Pip-Boy 3000 Terminal**. Featuring **10 customizable Fallout faction & holiday themes**, smooth interactive button glows, dynamic resolution scaling, and **full bilingual support** (English & Turkish with instant one-click switching).

In addition to visual polish, this mod fixes critical engine crashes from legacy mods (such as the notorious freeze when leveling large vaults), introduces a built-in background **Quest Automation engine**, and natively integrates **V.A.T.S. (Vault Accelerated Time System)** for instant time control with smart auto-pausing.

## Installation instructions

### For Most Users (Full Pack - Recommended):
1. Download the **Full Pack** from the Files tab.
2. Open your Fallout Shelter root game directory:
   `<Default: C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Fallout Shelter>`
3. Extract all contents of the zip file directly into the game folder (so that `winhttp.dll` and the `BepInEx` folder sit next to `FalloutShelter.exe`).
4. Launch the game through Steam!

### For Users Who Already Have BepInEx Installed:
1. Download the **Plugins Only** archive.
2. Extract the contents directly into your game's `BepInEx/plugins` folder.

---

## How to Use in Game:
* Press **F4** on your keyboard to toggle the mod menu at any time.
* Alternatively, click the **[ ⚙ MOD MENU (F4) ]** terminal button located just below your dweller count icon in the top left.
* Click **LANG: EN / DİL: TR** in the top-right corner of the menu to swap languages instantly.
* Click **◄** or **►** on the theme bar to switch between the 10 Fallout faction themes.
* Use the **V.A.T.S.** tab to toggle time acceleration, or use `Ctrl + Alt` / `Shift + Alt`.

---

## Uninstallation:
Delete `BepInEx`, `winhttp.dll`, `doorstop_config.ini`, and `.doorstop_version` from your game directory. Your game will return to 100% vanilla.
